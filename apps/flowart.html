Test what the site has

Static pressure, dynamic pressure and timed flow at the point of connection. The app works out what this site can genuinely run, before anything is drawn.

Draw the areas

Trace lawn, beds and planters on the site plan and group them into hydrozones by water demand, sun and soil.

Place the heads

Choose the range you install and the app lays out head spacing and arcs, checking head-to-head coverage across every zone.

Let it size everything

Pipe diameter run by run, precipitation match per zone, stations grouped inside the available flow — and a booster pump specification if the site comes up short.

Take the list and the price

Every head, valve, fitting and metre of pipe with part numbers, straight into a supplier order and a priced quote.

Questions

Does it design drip as well as spray?

Yes. Drip line spacing, emitter rate and run length are handled alongside spray and rotor zones, including mixed systems where beds are drip and lawn is spray.

What if the site doesn't have enough pressure?

The app tells you early, then designs around it — smaller zones, different nozzle selection, or a booster pump specified to the flow and head the design needs.

Is it tied to one brand?

No. Nozzle and head data covers the ranges commonly available in Australia, and you set which supplier and brand you work with. Part numbers come out matching what you actually order.

Can I hand the design to the client?

Yes. The plan, zone schedule and watering program export as a client document, separate from your costings and supplier pricing.

When can I use it?

FlowArt is in build. Early access opens to a limited group first, with pricing announced before anyone is charged.
