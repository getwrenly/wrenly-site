Questions

Does it work without signal on site?

The measuring, set-out and calculator tools work offline. Supplier pricing, council data and client notifications sync when you're back in range.

Which councils are covered?

New South Wales metropolitan and surrounding councils at launch, with the rest of Australia rolled out by state. The DCP pathway resolves from the job address, so you get the right document, not a general one.

Can my clients see everything?

No. You choose what's published to the portal — brief, concept, documents, milestones and payment requests. Your costings, supplier pricing and internal notes stay on your side.

Do I need to move off my current job software?

No. Plenty of businesses will use Landscape Mate for the site and design work and keep invoicing where it is. Exports are plain files, not a lock-in.

When can I use it?

Landscape Mate is in build. Early access opens to a limited group first, with pricing announced before anyone is charged.
